3. Important decisions for our payment dataset
We should not automatically delete or replace data without understanding its meaning.

Examples:
- Missing Merchant ID: investigate; don't invent an ID.
- Missing Transaction Amount: flag the record.
- Duplicate ID: investigate before removing.
- Negative transaction amounts: determine whether they represent refunds or reversals.
- Different payment status formats: standardise formatting without changing the actual status meaning.
- Missing Surcharge: determine whether it means zero or unknown.
We will preserve the original raw CSV and create a separate cleaned dataset.
